WindowHop, AltTab or the Mac’s own ⌘Tab?

All three are good at different things. The Mac’s App Switcher is built in and switches apps. AltTab, which WindowHop comes from, switches windows with many options. WindowHop switches windows with almost no setup. If one of them already works for you, keep it.

Which one fits you

Choose by how you work.

Keep the Mac’s ⌘Tab if

  • You keep one window per app most of the time.
  • You want nothing to install and no permission to give.

Choose WindowHop if

  • You keep several windows of the same app open.
  • You want ⌘Tab to reach windows, with nearly no settings.
  • You want no Screen Recording permission, no telemetry and no paid tier.
  • You have an Apple silicon Mac with macOS 26 or later.

Choose AltTab if

  • You want to search windows by typing (Pro).
  • You want more styles, sizes and shortcuts.
  • You have an Intel Mac or a macOS version older than macOS 26.

WindowHop and AltTab are free and open source under GPL-3.0. WindowHop keeps AltTab’s attribution and history, and credits Louis Pontoise (lwouis) and the AltTab contributors.
